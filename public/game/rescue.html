    FIELD GUIDE

    You lead. They help.

    Walk with W A S D or the arrow keys. Press E / Space near an object, or use the action button. Location buttons also move you there, including on a phone.

    Ask Mia to repair the bridge, then ask her to hold the power switch while you turn the crank. Ask Leo for the ship’s signal. Finish by aiming the light.

    先指挥 Mia 修桥；再让她按住供电开关，你来转动摇柄。问 Leo 船的信号，然后控制灯塔方向。卡住时用快捷指令，随时打开中文提示；没有倒计时惩罚。

    English phrases: Can you fix…? · Hold the switch. · What’s the signal? · Aim left / right.

    Mia and Leo are game characters. Mission actions are simulated locally. If the server has an AI connection, typed messages receive live AI English replies; it does not listen to your microphone.
